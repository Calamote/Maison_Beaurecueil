Overview

A Provençal house at the foot of Sainte-Victoire

Located in Beaurecueil, about 10 km from Aix-en-Provence, this 110 m² old Provençal house offers a quiet, green setting, ideal for holidays with family or friends.

The house has plenty of character, with thick old walls, tasteful decoration and a naturally cool atmosphere in summer. The flower-filled garden, shaded terrace and pool make it a lovely place to relax and enjoy Provence.

The house is semi-detached, but well protected by greenery and a tall bamboo hedge, with no direct overlooking. The atmosphere is peaceful, private and perfect for unwinding.

The house

Old-world charm, natural coolness and easy summer living

The house has 3 bedrooms: two bedrooms with 160 cm beds and one bedroom with a 140 cm bed. It also includes a living room, a separate equipped kitchen, a bathroom, and an old vaulted room with exposed stone and a sofa bed, especially cool in summer.

Each bedroom has a fan. There is no air conditioning, but the thick walls and layout help keep the house naturally cool during hot summer days.

Outside, you can enjoy a terrace, a green flower-filled garden, outdoor furniture, a hammock, sun loungers and a private semi-in-ground pool around 1.30 m deep.

Around the house

Between nature, Aix and Sainte-Victoire

The house is set in a quiet, green environment, close to the small village of Beaurecueil and the landscapes of Sainte-Victoire.

Small local shops are available nearby, and a supermarket is around 4 km away. Aix-en-Provence is about 10 km away, so you can enjoy a very natural setting while staying close to services and the city.

There are many walks to enjoy around the house. An equestrian centre is about 700 m away. Beaches and the Calanques can be reached by car, including Cassis and the Mediterranean depending on traffic. The Luberon, Marseille and the lakes of Provence also make great day-trip ideas.
